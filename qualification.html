How these numbers are calculated

Every verdict here uses points only. A club is called eliminated only when the most points it can still reach is lower than the points 24 rivals are already assured of — so the claim holds in every remaining combination of results.

The “points to clinch” figure is deliberately pessimistic: it assumes every rival wins all of its remaining matches. Because rivals also play each other and cannot all win, the real figure is often lower. The number shown is a safe upper bound — it will never tell a club it is safe when it is not.

Tiebreakers such as goal difference only decide order between clubs on equal points, so they can never overturn a points-based verdict. Full detail is in the methodology, and you can test your own results in the simulator.
