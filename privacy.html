Privacy Policy

What this site stores

Your predicted scores and draw are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. They are never sent to, or stored on, any server operated by this site. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser will reset your predictions.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising served by third-party providers such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Visitors can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

This site may use standard web analytics to understand aggregate traffic. This data is not linked to any personally identifying information, because this site does not collect personally identifying information.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
