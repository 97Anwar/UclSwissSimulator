Champions League 2026/27 Pots & Seeding

Before the league-phase draw, the 36 clubs are seeded into four pots of nine, ranked by UEFA club coefficient. Pot 1 holds the highest-ranked sides (the reigning champions are placed in Pot 1); Pot 4 the lowest-ranked. In the draw, every club is paired with two opponents from each of the four pots — so even a Pot 1 side still has to face two other Pot 1 clubs.
