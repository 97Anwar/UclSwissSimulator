How Napoli got here

Napoli (🇮🇹) qualified for the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League and was placed in Pot 3 for the league-phase draw. In the 36-team Swiss-style league phase, every club plays eight different opponents — two drawn from each of the four pots — with the top eight going straight to the round of 16, teams 9th–24th entering the knockout play-offs, and 25th–36th eliminated.
