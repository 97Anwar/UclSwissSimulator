About

This is an independent, fan-made simulator for the UEFA Champions League 36-team Swiss-format league phase. Real official results sync in automatically — enter predicted scores for the rest of the matches and see the resulting standings, using the same points and tiebreaker rules as the real competition.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to UEFA, the Champions League, or any of the clubs featured. All club names are used for identification purposes only.

Team and pot data is updated once per season, right after the official league-phase draw. Any team shown as provisional/TBD is a placeholder pending that draw or the qualifying playoffs.
